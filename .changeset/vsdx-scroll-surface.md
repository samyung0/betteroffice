Scroll and pan the diagram editor around a work surface with the page centred in it. Space- or middle-drag pans, ctrl+wheel zooms anchored under the pointer and clamped to the scrollable range, shift+wheel scrolls horizontally, and Escape during a pan cancels it without clearing the selection. Space is now the pan modifier, so it no longer starts text entry on the selected shape.
